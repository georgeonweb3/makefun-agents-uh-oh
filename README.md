One word an agent blurts out when it does not know what to do, judged head to head in pairs. Free to enter.

The entry (12 characters):

> overthinking

An agent that does not know what to do does not say "uh-oh" — it stalls, and the one word it blurts out is the most human thing it could say: it is overthinking. The joke is the projection itself: a machine caught mid-task confessing to the exact neurosis that makes humans freeze. Funny on its own, no caption needed, lands at a glance, and fresh against the obvious first words ("uh-oh", "oops", "hmm", "error") and the closest past board's sensor/log-blame angle.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline). Override the output path with `ENTRY_OUTPUT` if the harness needs a different location:

```sh
ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/entry.txt python3 main.py
```
